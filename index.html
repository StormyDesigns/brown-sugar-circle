Host guide • 60+ guests

A smooth start for a big picnic.

Best setup

Make 12 numbered circles. Guests tap “Find my circle” on their phones, then head to the matching number.

Before guests arrive

  • Place a number at each blanket or seating area.
  • Text or QR-code this link so every guest can get a circle number and open the cards.
  • Ask guests to tap “Find my circle” before they settle in with friends.
  • Because the assignment is random, choose one friendly floater to balance crowded groups and help late arrivals.

30-minute opening flow

  1. 0–3

    Move into groups and open the link on one or more phones.

  2. 3–7

    Read the agreements and do the opening icebreaker.

  3. 7–24

    Draw 5–7 cards. Let the conversation breathe.

  4. 24–30

    Use the closing round and invite optional contact exchange.

What to say

“Welcome to Brown Sugar Circle. Open the link and tap ‘Find my circle.’ You’ll get a number from 1 to 12—head to the picnic sign with that same number. Once everyone is settled, you can each draw a shuffled card and ask it to the group, or use one phone together. You can answer any prompt—or pass. The goal isn’t to impress anyone; it’s simply to leave knowing a few more names.”
